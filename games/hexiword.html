HOW TO PLAY

Hexiword is a competitive territory control game. The goal is to capture 7 Hexicons before your opponent.

  • Playing Words

    Swipe adjacent Neutral or Your Own tiles to spell words. You cannot use your opponent's tiles.

    W
    I
    N
  • Swapping Letters

    Long-press (hold) any uncaptured tile until it glows purple, then drag it to swap. (Free action once per turn!)

  • Capturing Hexicons

    When a tile and its 6 surrounding neighbors are completely played, whoever owns the Majority (4+) permanently captures the center tile!
